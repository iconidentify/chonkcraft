# Workers Keep Their Cargo

- Fixed peons and peasants changing between wood and gold bags when redirected
  between trees and mines. A loaded worker keeps its cargo.
- Unfinished chopping no longer appears as a completed load or enables Return
  Goods. Starting a new harvesting job clears that unfinished work.
- Fixed loaded workers ignoring movement redirects during delivery, and
  restored the Battle.net Edition pause before a gold worker's next trip.
